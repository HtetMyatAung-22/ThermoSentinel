# ThermoSentinel v0.2 — Conference Demo Script

## 90-second demonstration

1. Open **Conference Demo**.
2. Press **1 · Routine**.
   - Say: “This represents normal site behavior. The predictive layer is intentionally simulated because real continuous ground sensors have not yet been installed.”
3. Press **2 · Developing Hotspot**.
   - Say: “As temperature and CO begin changing together, the system moves from simple monitoring toward a Watch condition.”
4. Press **3 · Thermal Escalation**.
   - Say: “The system now prioritizes Zone C and estimates a high 12-hour thermal-escalation probability. This is not a fire probability.”
5. Open **Live NASA FIRMS**.
   - Say: “This component is real near-real-time satellite evidence from NASA FIRMS. The live feed is kept separate from simulated predictive data.”
6. If no nearby detection appears:
   - Say: “No NRT VIIRS detection observed does not mean no fire. That limitation is one of the central findings of our historical study.”
7. Return to the conference-demo map.
   - Say: “The objective is to tell a technician where to inspect, when, why the system is concerned, and what evidence needs field confirmation.”

## Never say
- “This AI predicts landfill fires.”
- “78% chance of fire.”
- “This system is validated.”
- “No FIRMS detection means there is no fire.”

## Say instead
- “Prototype thermal-escalation forecast.”
- “Simulated ground-sensor prediction.”
- “Live NASA FIRMS satellite evidence.”
- “Prospective field validation is the next stage.”
